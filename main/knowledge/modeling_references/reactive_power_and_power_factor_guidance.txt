Reactive power and power factor guidance for GridSenpAI modeling retrieval.
Use this file when planner outputs require steady-state MVAr expectations, operating-point power factor, or bounded assumptions around reactive support and controls.

Focus retrieval on:
- requested peak-demand power factor and MVAr values from interconnection materials
- transformer and UPS topology that may affect reactive behavior at the POI
- capacitor banks, reactors, static var devices, or inverter-based reactive controls where present
- operating mode notes that distinguish normal, bypass, commissioning, or contingency behavior

Planner-output relevance:
- steady_state.q_mvar should track applicant-provided operating-point expectations whenever available
- power-factor values should remain tied to the referenced operating scenario or stage, not treated as globally fixed if documents indicate phase-dependent buildout
- when only bounded guidance is available, preserve that the value is inferred or assumed rather than confirmed

Evidence preference:
1. applicant interconnection forms and POI planning documents
2. applicant equipment/control narratives that materially affect reactive behavior
3. modeling references and bounded planning guidance
4. interview clarification when the operating-point expectation remains ambiguous
