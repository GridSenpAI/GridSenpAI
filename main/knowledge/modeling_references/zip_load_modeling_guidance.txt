ZIP load modeling guidance for GridSenpAI retrieval.

Legacy note:
ZIP load models are commonly used to represent composite load behavior.

The model consists of three components:

Z: constant impedance portion
I: constant current portion
P: constant power portion

Typical data center loads trend toward high constant-power fractions
due to switch-mode power supplies and UPS systems.

Planner-output relevance:
- zip_model.constant_power_fraction is often dominant for UPS-backed and switch-mode power-supply data center loads.
- zip_model.constant_current_fraction and zip_model.constant_impedance_fraction should only be populated from evidence, bounded assumptions, or review-approved modeling guidance.
- When UPS topology, battery support mode, bypass configuration, or transformer arrangement materially change load behavior, preserve rationale and confidence with the ZIP split.

Evidence preference:
1. applicant UPS topology and electrical design documents
2. vendor UPS references and sequence-of-operation notes
3. modeling references for typical bounded assumptions
4. interview clarification if the composite ZIP split remains uncertain
