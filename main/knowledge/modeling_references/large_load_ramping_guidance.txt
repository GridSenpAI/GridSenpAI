Large-load ramping guidance for GridSenpAI modeling retrieval.

Legacy note:
Large data center loads typically ramp in phases corresponding to buildout.

Typical characteristics include:

- phased MW increments
- ramp envelopes tied to equipment energization
- step changes during commissioning

Planner-output relevance:
- steady_state.p_mw and steady_state.q_mvar should reflect the requested operating point or stage-specific load block.
- ramping.max_ramp_up_mw_per_min should be bounded using commissioning phasing, staged equipment energization, and applicant-provided load commissioning plans.
- ramping.max_ramp_down_mw_per_min should reflect the credible unloading or trip-down envelope, not an invented symmetric assumption.

Evidence preference:
1. applicant load commissioning plan and staged MW schedule
2. applicant one-line / sequence of operation / controls narrative
3. modeling references and planner guidance
4. interview clarification if the ramp envelope remains unclear
