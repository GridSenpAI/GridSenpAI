Protection and controls guidance for GridSenpAI retrieval.
Use this file when interconnection sufficiency or planner review depends on relay behavior, transfer-trip logic, reconnect logic, transformer protection, or cooling/load-shed controls.

Focus retrieval on:
- undervoltage / overvoltage / underfrequency / overfrequency trip settings
- transfer-trip and direct-transfer-trip behavior
- automatic reconnection or reclose blocking logic
- transformer high-side / low-side protection summaries
- protection communications and permissive/blocking channels
- cooling disconnect / reconnect sequencing when material to MW step behavior
- measurement basis notes such as per-phase, RMS, aggregate, or relay element basis

Planner-facing guidance:
- protection settings should be exported with provenance and review notes, not normalized into unsupported single values when documents disagree
- reconnect logic and transfer-trip assumptions can materially affect dynamic and ramping interpretations
- missing relay detail should remain unresolved or review-required rather than inferred from equipment family alone

Evidence preference:
1. applicant protection package and relay settings summaries
2. one-line and controls narrative in applicant packet
3. interconnection/planner guidance
4. interview clarification for unresolved trip/reconnect logic
