GridSenpAI interconnection checklist notes for retrieval and gap resolution.
When interconnection-critical fields are missing, prioritize applicant packet evidence before interview:
- service_delivery_point_voltage_kv
- closest_sswg_bus_number or equivalent bus mapping
- requested_peak_demand_mw and power factor
- lcp_stage_schedule / energization phasing
- telemetry_and_metering_requirements
- protection_diagrams / relay settings summary
- site plan and one-line path from utility source to major load blocks

If these cannot be extracted from applicant artifacts, preserve them as missing or review-required. Do not fabricate interconnection values from vendor equipment specs.
