Telemetry and metering guidance for GridSenpAI retrieval.
Use this file when planner-facing outputs or interconnection review depend on operational visibility, metering context, or energization readiness signals.

Focus retrieval on:
- telemetry provider, contact, and communications path
- SCADA / EMS visibility points required for operations
- MW, MVAr, voltage, breaker status, and major load-block point mappings
- operations-model references and naming/mnemonic details when present
- metering ownership, metering voltage level, and interval/settlement context when supplied
- staged energization dates, production-load date, and operations handoff milestones

Planner-facing implications:
- missing telemetry visibility should remain review-required rather than assumed complete
- operations-model point mapping belongs in planner packet notes when present
- metering and telemetry requirements can affect energization readiness even when vendor equipment specs are complete

Evidence preference:
1. applicant telemetry / operations-model package
2. utility or ISO requirements present in applicant packet
3. interconnection guidance and planner checklists
4. interview clarification for unresolved point mapping, provider, or readiness details
